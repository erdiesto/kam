Support

Having a problem or a question? Write to [DESTEK E-POSTASI].

My purchases are missing: tap "Restore purchases" on the title screen or on the screen at the end of floor 1, signed in with the same store account.

Refunds go through the store: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, your order history in the Play Store for Google Play.

Saves are kept on your device only; deleting the game deletes them. Purchases belong to your store account and can be restored.
